Privacy Policy

Macrobook · Last updated October 18, 2026

Macrobook is a small calorie and macro tracking app run by an individual, not a company. This page explains what information the app handles and why.

What we collect

  • Sign-in information: your email address, and if you sign in with Google or Discord, the name and profile picture that service shares with us.
  • Profile and goals: sex, birthdate, height, activity level, weight goal, and units you choose.
  • Your tracking data: weights you log, foods and meals you log, exercise you log, and foods or recipes you add.
  • Cookbook recipe facts: when you import a cookbook PDF, we store recipe names, serving sizes, nutrition numbers, and (for recipes without printed numbers) ingredient lists. We do not store the PDF file, page images, directions, or photos. The PDF is processed temporarily and deleted. A copy of the file you imported stays only in your own browser, on your device.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: calculating your calorie goals, showing your diary and trends, and finding foods. We do not sell your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking services.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase stores accounts and data and handles sign-in.
  • Render and Vercel host the app.
  • Google and Discord provide optional sign-in, if you choose them.
  • Anthropic (the maker of Claude) receives limited text to power two features: the name of a restaurant item when you ask the app to look it up, and the text of cookbook pages the app could not read automatically when you import a cookbook. Your name, email, weight, and diary are never sent to Anthropic.

Your data and your choices

  • You can edit or remove your logs, cookbooks, and foods in the app at any time.
  • To delete your account and all of its data, contact the person who runs this app, using the support email shown on the sign-in screen, and it will be removed.
  • Your sign-in session and a copy of any cookbook PDFs you import are kept in your browser's storage on your device. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Children

Macrobook is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

Calorie targets, estimates, and nutrition numbers in Macrobook are for general information only. They can be inaccurate, and they are not medical or dietary advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making major changes to your diet, especially if you have a medical condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
